# Senate Accountability Briefs

This folder helps you prepare an accountability brief and a cover email for each senator, ahead of the Senate vote on the NDIS Amendment (Securing the NDIS for Future Generations) Bill 2026 — so senators hear, in local and human terms, what this Bill could mean for the people in their own state before they vote on it.

You don't need to be technical to use this. You do need to actually read what comes out of it before it's sent — nothing here is meant to go to a senator's office without a person checking it first.

## What "done" looks like

For each senator you're contacting, you end up with two files, ready to send:

- **A Word document** — the full brief: that senator's state data, funding exposure, and the questions their office should expect after the vote.
- **A cover email** — plain text you can paste straight into your email client, with the brief attached.

## Before you start

- **If you have access to an AI agent that can run code** (like this session), it can do almost all of the work for you — pulling the data, doing the maths, filling in the Word template. You mostly just need to check its work and add your own voice.
- **If you're working in a plain chat window with no code access** (ChatGPT, Claude.ai chat, etc.), that's fine too — see [`01_reference/chat-only-workflow.md`](01_reference/chat-only-workflow.md) for how to do this by pasting files into the chat instead.

Either way, the four things you'll actually be asked to do as a human are:

1. **Check the data** a senator's brief was built from is correct.
2. **Confirm whether that senator holds a relevant portfolio** (e.g. Minister for Women) — this isn't automatic; it's a judgement call the system flags for you to make.
3. **Write a short personal note**, if you want one — your own words on why this matters to you, or answer a few questions and let the AI help you draft it. This is the part that turns numbers into something a senator's office can't wave away.
4. **Read the final brief and email before you send them.**

## Getting started

Tell whoever/whatever you're working with (an AI agent, or yourself in a chat window) that you want to prepare a brief for a senator — by name, by party, by state, or all of them — and it will take you through the steps from there. The full routing details and folder-by-folder explanation live in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md); you shouldn't need to read it yourself unless you're curious how it works under the hood.

## A note on tone

This isn't about attacking senators, and it isn't an argument against reforming the NDIS. It's about making sure that if this Bill causes harm once it's implemented, the people who had the power to ask harder questions and demand safeguards before voting can't say they didn't know. Keep that framing in your personal note too — specific and human, not accusatory.
