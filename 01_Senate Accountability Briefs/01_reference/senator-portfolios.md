## Borderline cases worth a second look

The automated check is a keyword match, not judgment. These didn't hit a keyword but might still be worth a human look before you write them off:

- **the Hon Nita Louise Green** — Assistant Minister for Northern Australia touches regional development, though not quite the "Regional Australia / Regional Health" category as written (which is about regional *service availability*, not economic development). Borderline.
- **the Hon Donald Edward Farrell** — Minister for Trade and Tourism. Not one of the approved categories, but if you think a Trade/Tourism angle is worth adding to `portfolio-relevance.md` as a new category, raise it before using it on a brief (don't invent categories ad hoc per-senator).
- Whip/Leader/Manager-of-Business roles (marked "procedural role only" above) are Senate procedural positions, not portfolios — they don't get an accountability lens under the current rule, but they're real influence (e.g. party Whips control internal party discipline on votes) worth remembering when deciding who else to prioritise contacting.
